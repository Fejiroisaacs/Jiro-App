ROOT (Journaly - The Reflection Space)
 ├──  Home Dashboard (The Feed)
 │    ├──  Streak Banner Display (Top)
 │    │
 │    ├──  Tab 1: Private Journal
 │    │    ├── Calendar View (Highlight active days)
 │    │    ├── Private Entry Stream
 │    │    └── Filters (Mood, Tag, Date range)
 │    │
 │    ├──  Tab 2: Groups (Shared Journals)
 │    │    ├── List of Groups
 │    │    ├── Create New Group
 │    │    └── Group Detail View (Feed of all members' entries)
 │    │         ├── Manage Members (Invite via Email)
 │    │         └── Post to Group
 │    │
 │    └──  Tab 3: Collections (Thematic Grouping)
 │         ├── Grid of Collections (Cover image, count)
 │         ├── Create New Collection
 │         └── Collection Detail View (Entries assigned to collection)
 │
 ├──  Entry Editor (The Canvas)
 │    ├──  Write Body Content
 │    ├──  Mood Picker (Horizontal scroll of 8 mood chips)
 │    ├──  Tag Input (Type-and-enter chips)
 │    ├──  Image Uploads (Up to 3 per entry)
 │    └──  Assign to Collection (Multi-select dropdown)
 │
 └──  Quick Access (Floating/Bottom Nav)
      ├── Home
      ├── Groups
      └── New Entry FAB (Floating Action Button)
